Window offers comparison

8 suppliers, latest offer from each, read line by line and checked against the system makers' published data. Prepared 27 October 2026, updated the same day with Altunoğlu. Reference: Teknik Şartname Rev. 2 (13 Oct) plus Ahmed's Rev. 3 notes (19 Oct). All prices excluding KDV.

Bottom line, from a luxury-build point of view
  • Best product for this house: the Alumil package (Bornes or Altunoğlu). It is the only system on the table whose sliding door is rated for the 400 kg salon panel with margin (up to 600 kg with Alumil's lift unit), with a 24 mm thermal break on both window and door and a published Uw of 1.1 on the door. Altunoğlu's R3 drawings are the only supplier drawings that already follow your 19 Oct layout exactly; Bornes priced on the earlier R1 set with your Rev. 3 elevations as reference.
  • Best price: Vemsa (~€81K like-for-like). Good Turkish system (Asaş), nice single-track detail on the sliders, but the lift-slide hardware it names is a 300 kg class, and the door profile has a 14.8 mm thermal break. Fixable, at a cost.
  • Bornes (~€90K) is the value pick. About €9K over Vemsa for a clearly better sliding door. Altunoğlu quotes the same Alumil systems directly at ~€99K like-for-like, with a brighter low-iron glass but no jalousie, and with a side note proposing a part-unofficial payment that you should decline.
  • Delta (~€86K) quotes a Burak system on the old layout with 300 kg hardware. Asimetri (~€91K) has now sent drawings: they show the old 28-unit layout and a sliding system labelled "standard 300 kg". Referans (~€129K) is 50% over and splits your big panes. Fatihpen and Haser are not comparable.
  • Nobody is ready to sign. No supplier gives seaside paint treatment, warranties, lead time, or the maximum size of the between-glass blinds. The blinds at P07 and P13 are likely tilt-only at those sizes, and P13's top pane may be over the limit.
  • Decision for you: the spec's glass (50/33) is a darker, more reflective office-type glass. Altunoğlu's 62/44 low-iron is brighter and more neutral, at a small cost in summer heat. For a view house I would switch and ask everyone to quote it.

Fine print, supplier by supplier

Altunoğlu Cephe (Alumil), 27 Oct, €97,500

  • Payment note on the offer: invoice €65,000 and take €32,500 "gayri resmi", total cash €110,500 instead of €117,000 with full KDV. Do not accept. The uninvoiced part has no warranty or legal standing, KDV on a registered construction business is normally recoverable anyway (check with your accountant), and it creates tax exposure for you.
  • Priced per position on its own R3 unit drawings (19 Oct), which match your layout: 2A/2B, 7A/7B, 8A/8B, 13A/13B, 14A/14B mirrored, one arrow per sliding door, 26 units, 293 m². 22 window units €59,181, 4 sliding doors €48,081, then a 9% discount.
  • Sliders drawn with "continuous sash profile, standard sill rail": the fixed half is framed as a sash, not glazed direct. Slightly heavier look than Vemsa's single-track detail.
  • Includes double-row galvanised körkasa and EPDM membranes. Excludes crane (not mentioned), scaffolding, heavy steel at parapets, special hardware such as closers or electronic locks.
  • Glass is not the spec's: 62/44 solar low-e plus a low-iron (extra clear) laminate. Better looking, see the glass section. "Any change to the glass changes the price."
  • No jalousie anywhere in the offer. No smart glass. "Standard colours": confirm anthracite RAL 7016 is one.
  • Remeasure after installation. Valid 10 days (to 6 Nov).

The glass decision

Spec: Şişecam Ecosol T 50 (50/33)

  • Temperable solar-control low-e. About 49% daylight, g 0.33, U 1.1 with 16 mm argon.
  • Blocks the most summer heat. Looks greyer from inside and more mirror-like from outside. Şişecam lists it for offices, hotels and hospitals.
  • Quoted by Vemsa, Delta, Bornes, Asimetri, Referans.

Altunoğlu: Şişecam 62/44 low-e, low-iron laminate

  • Same family, about 62% daylight, g 0.44, U about 1.1. Şişecam lists it for residential.
  • Brighter rooms, truer colours, less reflection. The low-iron inner laminate removes the green edge tint, a detail you see on 3 m panes.
  • About a third more solar gain than 50/33. On the south and west glass that is real in July; the between-glass blinds and the roof overhang are what control it.

My view for Verde: the view and the feel of the rooms are the product. Switch the spec to 62/44 with a low-iron laminate and ask every shortlisted supplier to price it. Expect €10-20/m² more than 50/33.

Recommendation

  • Run a final round with Bornes, Vemsa and Delta, all on Rev. 3, 62/44 low-iron glass, 400 kg+ hardware named, seaside coating, crane and EPDM in, körkasa out, flush threshold as an option. Keep Asimetri in only if it comes back with a 400 kg+ slider and an inclusion list.
  • Target: Bornes at about €85-88K like-for-like. Vemsa's €81K is the lever; Bornes's euro price and better door are the reasons to pay a little more.
  • Altunoğlu: ask first how it relates to Bornes. If it fabricates for Bornes, there is no point paying ~€9K more to go direct. If it is a separate Alumil fabricator, ask for a fully invoiced price with jalousie, and it becomes a second Alumil bidder against Bornes.
  • Before signing anyone: visit one finished villa of theirs with a 3 m lift-slide door, and see a corner sample in anthracite. Corner joints and the meeting stile are where cheap work shows.
  • Close Referans, Haser and Fatihpen with thanks.
