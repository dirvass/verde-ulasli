Fill-in prices: crane €2,157 (Delta, 2 days), EPDM €5,510 (Delta, 352 m), körkasa €8,635 (Delta). €/m² on the spec's 293 m² frame area. The ranking holds at any USD rate between 1.10 and 1.20. WIRUS goods alone, both villas, are €139K: 1.7× Bornes all-in.

Bottom line, from a luxury-build point of view
  • Best product: Alumil (Bornes or Altunoğlu). The only sliding door rated for the 400 kg salon panel, 24 mm thermal break, Uw 1.1.
  • Cheapest: Vemsa (~€73K). Good Asaş system, but 300 kg hardware and a weaker door profile.
  • Value pick: Bornes (~€82K). €9K over Vemsa for a clearly better door. Altunoğlu is the same product at ~€91K, plus an unofficial-payment note to decline.
  • Delta, Asimetri: old layout, 300 kg hardware. Referans: ~50% over, splits the big panes. Fatihpen, Haser: not comparable.
  • WIRUS (Germany): about twice the field. €139K for the goods alone, before delivery and installation. Strong German product with triple glass, but it priced one villa, and its sliders stop at 3.0 m high.
  • Nobody is ready to sign: no seaside coating, warranties or lead time from anyone.

The glass decision

Spec: Şişecam Ecosol T 50 (50/33)

  • Temperable solar-control low-e. About 49% daylight, g 0.33, U 1.1 with 16 mm argon.
  • Blocks the most summer heat. Looks greyer from inside and more mirror-like from outside. Şişecam lists it for offices, hotels and hospitals.
  • Quoted by Vemsa, Delta, Bornes, Asimetri, Referans.

Altunoğlu: Şişecam 62/44 low-e, low-iron laminate

  • Same family, about 62% daylight, g 0.44, U about 1.1. Şişecam lists it for residential.
  • Brighter rooms, truer colours, less reflection. The low-iron inner laminate removes the green edge tint, a detail you see on 3 m panes.
  • About a third more solar gain than 50/33. On the south and west glass that is real in July; the roof overhang and external shading are what control it.

WIRUS: triple glass, laminated both sides

  • VSG 8 / 12 / 4-6 / 12-14 / VSG 8 with black warm edge. Sun-protection codes 46/25 and 47/26: about 46% daylight, g about 0.25. Ug 0.6-0.7, about 39 dB.
  • Darker than the spec's 50/33 and much darker than 62/44. Best heat, noise and break-in protection. About 55 kg/m², half again the spec's 36 kg/m²: heavier sashes, heavier crane work.
  • For Gölcük's climate triple glass is more than needed. It is a large part of the price gap, though not all of it.

My view for Verde: the view and the feel of the rooms are the product. Switch the spec to 62/44 with a low-iron laminate and ask every shortlisted supplier to price it. Expect €10-20/m² more than 50/33.
